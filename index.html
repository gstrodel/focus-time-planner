How to import into Outlook

After saving: in Outlook on the web, choose Calendar → Add calendar → Upload from file, select the .ics, choose your main work calendar, then import. Only new approved future blocks are exported; focus already on Outlook is excluded. This is a one-time import. Reimporting may create duplicates; later edits or removals in this app do not update imported appointments.
